Mantra
Saturn Beej Mantra
Chant "Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namaha" to pacify Saturn's challenging aspects and strengthen discipline.
108 repetitions every Saturday morning, ideally before sunrise. Use a rudraksha mala.
🌿
Mantra
Mercury Mantra
Chant "Om Bum Budhaya Namaha" during Mercury antardasha to sharpen intellect and communication.
27 repetitions every Wednesday. Best done in a green or natural setting.
🌅
Ritual
Surya Arghya
Offer water to the rising sun each morning. Strengthens Sun's positive influence in Aries and supports career visibility.
Fill a copper vessel with water and a few drops of rose water. Offer while chanting "Om Hram Hreem Hraum Sah Suryaya Namaha".

Practice
Saturday Fast
Observe a partial or full fast every Saturday during Saturn Mahadasha. Eat sesame seeds (til) or urad dal.
Saturn rules Saturday. Fasting reduces ego and builds discipline — qualities Saturn rewards.
🤝
Charitable Act
Feed the Hungry
Donate food (especially black sesame, mustard oil, or dark lentils) to the underprivileged every Saturday.
Saturn governs the masses and service. Giving food alleviates Saturn's karmic debts and opens abundance channels.
🌸
Charitable Act
Venus Offering
Gift white clothes, sugar, or rice to young women or elderly women in your community on Fridays.
Prepares for the powerful Venus antardasha arriving in 2027. Activates 11th-house abundance.
🔥
Practice
Hanuman Chalisa
Recite the Hanuman Chalisa on Tuesdays and Saturdays. Strengthens Mars and pacifies Ketu's unsettling energy.
Particularly useful during the 2026 Ketu antardasha period for mental clarity and protection.
🌼
Lifestyle
Jupiter Gratitude Practice
Keep a daily gratitude journal. Express genuine appreciation before meals. Strengthens Gajakesari Yoga's benefic effects.
Jupiter expands what you focus on. Gratitude aligns your consciousness with abundance frequencies. Practice for 21 days continuously.
