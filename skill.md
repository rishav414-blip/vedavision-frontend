# 1. CHART FOUNDATION

## 1.1 Ascendant (Lagna) — Chart Ruler

The Lagna is the single most important point in the chart. It governs:
- Physical body, constitution, and vitality
- Overall life direction and personality
- The lens through which all other house matters are experienced

**Calculation priority:**
1. Determine precise Lagna (rising sign) from birth time, date, and place
2. Lagna lord's placement, dignity, and aspects determine the native's overall strength and life trajectory
3. A strong Lagna lord (in own sign, exalted, in kendra/trikona, aspected by benefics) = stronger overall life outcomes
4. A weak Lagna lord (debilitated, in dusthana, combust, aspected by malefics) = challenges requiring remediation

## 1.2 Reading Rashi (D1) and Navamsa (D9) Together

**Rashi chart (D1)** = physical world manifestation, events, outer life
**Navamsa chart (D9)** = soul's inner truth, the chart that "confirms" or "denies" D1 promises

**Rules for D1 + D9 synthesis:**
- A planet strong in D1 but weak in D9 = outer success with inner dissatisfaction; promises partially delivered
- A planet weak in D1 but strong in D9 = slow start, eventual fulfillment after midlife; hidden strength
- A planet strong in both D1 and D9 = full and reliable manifestation of that planet's significations
- A planet weak in both D1 and D9 = that signification is genuinely challenged; remedies strongly advised
- **Vargottama** planets (same sign in D1 and D9) carry exceptional strength regardless of sign quality
- Check D9 Lagna lord — its strength indicates the quality of the latter half of life (especially post-marriage)

**Navamsa reading sequence:**
1. Identify D9 Lagna and its lord
2. Locate all planets — note those that are exalted/debilitated/own-sign in D9
3. Cross-reference key D1 yogas to see if D9 confirms them
4. For marriage: 7th house in D9, Venus in D9, Darakaraka planet in D9

Each planet's debilitation is exactly opposite its exaltation sign.
- Sun: Tula | Moon: Vrishchika | Mars: Karka | Mercury: Meena | Jupiter: Makara | Venus: Kanya | Saturn: Mesha

**Neecha Bhanga (Cancellation of Debilitation):**
Debilitation is canceled (and often reverses to strength) when:
1. The lord of the sign of debilitation is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon
2. The planet that would be exalted in that sign is in a kendra from Lagna or Moon
3. The debilitated planet is aspected by its own sign lord
4. The debilitated planet is in mutual aspect or conjunction with an exalted planet

### Own Sign (Swakshetra)

# 2. CAREER & WEALTH INDICATORS

## 2.1 Career Analysis (10th House System)

**Primary indicators in order of importance:**
1. **10th house lord** — its sign, nakshatra, house placement, dignity, and aspects
2. **Planets in 10th house** — any planet in the 10th directly shapes career expression
3. **Aspects on 10th house** — planets aspecting the 10th house influence career from a distance
4. **Sun placement** — natural karaka for authority and profession; Sun's sign and house modify career nature
5. **Saturn role** — as natural karaka of service, masses, and disciplined work; Saturn's condition shapes career longevity

**Reading the 10th lord:**
- 10th lord in 1st: Self-employed, personal brand, leadership roles
- 10th lord in 2nd: Finance, banking, family business, speech-based profession
- 10th lord in 3rd: Communication, media, sales, short-distance commerce
- 10th lord in 4th: Real estate, education, work from home, agriculture

- 10th lord in 6th: Service industry, law, medicine, military

- 10th lord in 12th: Foreign-based career, spiritual/hospital work, behind-the-scenes roles

## 2.2 Wealth Analysis (2nd and 11th Houses)

**2nd house = accumulated wealth, savings, family wealth**
- 2nd lord strong = capacity to accumulate
- Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, unafflicted Mercury) in 2nd = natural wealth
- Jupiter in 2nd = Dhana yoga (wealth combination)
- Malefics in 2nd without mitigation = fluctuating wealth, expenses on family

**11th house = income, gains, fulfillment**
- 11th lord strong = sustained income
- Jupiter in 11th = significant gains, especially during Jupiter Dasha/transit
- Rahu in 11th = unconventional but often large gains (especially in Rahu Dasha)
- Saturn in 11th = slow but steady accumulation; gains after age 30

**Key planets for wealth:**
- Jupiter: Natural karaka for wealth; its strength is non-negotiable for lasting prosperity
- Venus: Karaka for material comfort, luxury
- Mercury: Commerce, trade wealth
- 2nd and 11th lords together (by conjunction, aspect, or exchange) = strong Dhana yoga

# 3. DASHA SYSTEM (VIMSHOTTARI)

## 3.1 Full 120-Year Sequence

| Planet | Duration | Sequence Order |
|---|---|---|

Total: 120 years. The sequence repeats cyclically.

## 3.2 Calculating Mahadasha Start from Moon Nakshatra

**27 Nakshatras mapped to Dasha lords:**
| Nakshatra | Dasha Lord | Nakshatra | Dasha Lord | Nakshatra | Dasha Lord |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ashwini | Ketu | Magha | Ketu | Moola | Ketu |
| Bharani | Venus | Purva Phalguni | Venus | Purva Ashadha | Venus |
| Krittika | Sun | Uttara Phalguni | Sun | Uttara Ashadha | Sun |
| Rohini | Moon | Hasta | Moon | Shravana | Moon |
| Mrigashira | Mars | Chitra | Mars | Dhanishta | Mars |
| Ardra | Rahu | Swati | Rahu | Shatabhisha | Rahu |
| Punarvasu | Jupiter | Vishakha | Jupiter | Purva Bhadrapada | Jupiter |
| Pushya | Saturn | Anuradha | Saturn | Uttara Bhadrapada | Saturn |
| Ashlesha | Mercury | Jyeshtha | Mercury | Revati | Mercury |

**Calculation method:**
1. Identify Moon's nakshatra at birth
2. Identify the nakshatra lord — this is the first Mahadasha planet
3. Calculate how much of that nakshatra has elapsed: `elapsed° / total nakshatra span (13°20') × Dasha years = Dasha already elapsed at birth`
4. Remaining Dasha at birth = Total Dasha years − elapsed years
5. Subsequent Dashas follow the Vimshottari sequence in order

## 3.3 Antardasha (Sub-Period) Calculation

Each Mahadasha is divided into 9 Antardashas in the same Vimshottari sequence, starting with the Mahadasha lord itself.

**Formula:**
`Antardasha duration = (Mahadasha years × Antardasha planet years) / 120`

**Example — Saturn Mahadasha (19 years) Antardashas:**

|---|---|

| Ketu | 1 year 1 month 9 days |

| Sun | 11 months 12 days |

| Mars | 1 year 1 month 9 days |

## 3.4 Interpreting Dasha Lord + Antardasha Lord Combinations

**Framework for each combination:**
1. **Dasha lord's natal status:** House it rules, house it occupies, dignity, aspects received
2. **Antardasha lord's natal status:** Same assessment
3. **Relationship between the two:** Mutual friends/enemies? Do they rule complementary or conflicting houses?
4. **Houses activated:** Union of houses ruled by both planets = themes of the period

**Career/wealth timing — key combinations:**
- Mahadasha of 10th lord + Antardasha of 2nd/11th lord = career breakthrough with wealth
- Mahadasha of 9th lord + Antardasha of 10th lord = fortune meeting career
- Mahadasha of benefic in kendra + Antardasha of trikona lord = Raja yoga activation
- Mahadasha of 8th lord + Antardasha of 12th lord = loss, setback, transformation
- Mahadasha of debilitated planet (unmitigated) = struggle in signified areas
- Mahadasha of exalted planet = peak performance in signified areas

**General quality assessment:**
- Dasha lord as benefic, well-placed + Antardasha lord as benefic, well-placed = excellent period
- Dasha lord strong + Antardasha lord weak = mixed period, good intentions with obstacles
- Dasha lord weak + Antardasha lord strong = difficult overall tone, some relief
- Dasha lord weak + Antardasha lord weak = challenging; focus on remedies, patience

## 3.5 Pratyantar Dasha (Third Level — Precise Timing)

`Pratyantar duration = (Antardasha days × Pratyantar planet years) / 120`

Use Pratyantar Dasha for:
- Month-level timing of events
- Confirming whether a transit trigger will actually manifest
- Narrowing down within a favorable Antardasha which months are peak

# 4. TRANSIT ANALYSIS

## 4.1 Saturn Transits

**Sade Sati (7.5-year Saturn transit over natal Moon):**
- Phase 1 (Rising): Saturn transits the sign before natal Moon sign — mental stress, professional pressure builds
- Phase 2 (Peak): Saturn transits natal Moon sign — peak challenge; transformation of identity, finances, relationships
- Phase 3 (Setting): Saturn transits the sign after natal Moon sign — fatigue, gradual release, rebuilding

**Sade Sati effects by Moon sign** vary; the 12th, 1st, and 2nd transits from Moon are the three phases. Severity is modulated by:
- Saturn's natal strength (strong natal Saturn = milder Sade Sati)
- Whether Saturn rules a trikona for the Lagna (e.g., Saturn as 9th lord for Gemini Lagna = less harsh)
- Simultaneous Jupiter transit providing support

**Kantaka Shani (4th/7th/10th transit from Moon):**
- Saturn transiting 4th from natal Moon: Home disruption, mother's health, property issues
- Saturn transiting 7th from natal Moon: Relationship strain, partnership challenges
- Saturn transiting 10th from natal Moon: Career pressure, authority conflicts, reputation tested

**Ashtama Shani (Saturn in 8th from natal Moon):** Sudden events, health concerns, financial drain — requires extra caution.

## 4.2 Jupiter Transit (Annual Benefic Influence)

Jupiter moves approximately one sign per year (actual: ~12-13 months per sign).

**Beneficial Jupiter transits (from natal Moon):**
- Jupiter in 2nd from Moon: Wealth, family expansion, speech benefits
- Jupiter in 5th from Moon: Children, intelligence, fortune, creative success
- Jupiter in 7th from Moon: Marriage, partnerships, business expansion
- Jupiter in 9th from Moon: Fortune, travel, education, spiritual growth
- Jupiter in 11th from Moon: Significant gains, fulfillment of desires

**Challenging Jupiter transits:**
- Jupiter in 3rd from Moon: Hard work, sibling issues, some effort required
- Jupiter in 6th from Moon: Health attention, service themes
- Jupiter in 8th from Moon: Obstacles, hidden matters surface
- Jupiter in 12th from Moon: Expenditure, foreign matters, spiritual retreat

**Key rule:** Jupiter transiting the 1st, 5th, or 9th from Lagna simultaneously with favorable Moon-based transit = double benefic activation.

## 4.3 Rahu/Ketu 18-Month Transit Cycles

Rahu and Ketu transit in retrograde motion, approximately 18 months per sign (completing the zodiac in ~18 years).

**Transit effects (from Lagna and natal positions):**
- Rahu transiting 1st: Confusion about identity, foreign connections, unconventional changes
- Rahu transiting 2nd: Financial fluctuation, family dynamics, speech matters
- Rahu transiting 7th: Relationship upheaval, foreign partnerships
- Rahu transiting 10th: Career disruption/transformation, sudden reputation changes
- Rahu transiting 11th: Unusual but potentially large gains, network expansion
- Ketu transiting 5th: Spiritual growth, detachment from children/speculation
- Ketu transiting 9th: Guru changes, philosophical questioning

**Eclipse correlation:** Solar and lunar eclipses occur on the Rahu/Ketu axis. Natal planets within 5° of the eclipse degree are significantly activated — events related to those planets and houses they rule can manifest within 6 months of the eclipse.

## 4.4 Overlaying Transits on the Natal Chart

**Transit interpretation hierarchy:**
1. Transit over natal Moon (emotional/mental experience)
2. Transit over natal Sun (ego, vitality, career identity)
3. Transit over natal Lagna degree (physical manifestation)
4. Transit over natal planets (activation of that planet's significations)
5. Transit through natal houses (general house themes activated)

**Double transit rule (Parashari):** When both Jupiter and Saturn simultaneously aspect or transit the same house/planet, the effects are especially pronounced. A favorable double transit (both benefic to that point) = major positive manifestation. An unfavorable double transit = significant challenge.

# 5. FIVE-YEAR FORECAST METHODOLOGY

## Step 1: Identify Current and Upcoming Dasha/Antardasha Periods

1. Calculate exact Mahadasha from Moon nakshatra
2. Break down into Antardasha periods for the 5-year window
3. Assess each Dasha/Antardasha lord: dignity, house ruled, house occupied, natal aspects
4. Create a timeline: list each sub-period with start/end dates

## Step 2: Map Major Transit Triggers (Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu/Ketu)

For each of the 5 years:
- Note Saturn's transit sign and its relationship to natal Moon, Lagna, 10th house
- Note Jupiter's annual transit and house it activates
- Note which Rahu/Ketu axis is active and when it shifts
- Flag any eclipses on sensitive natal points

## Step 3: Year-by-Year Synthesis

For each year, combine:
- Active Mahadasha theme (background energy lasting years)
- Active Antardasha theme (foreground energy lasting months)
- Saturn transit quality (restraining/supportive)
- Jupiter transit quality (expanding/contracting)
- Rahu/Ketu axis effects

Weigh factors: Dasha carries 60% of the interpretive weight; transits carry 40%.

## Step 4: Flag Peak Opportunity Windows

A peak opportunity window is when 3 or more of the following converge:
- Antardasha of a strong, benefic, career/wealth-related planet
- Jupiter transiting a favorable position from Moon or Lagna (2nd, 5th, 9th, 11th)
- Saturn not in Sade Sati or transiting a supportive position
- A natal Raja/Dhana yoga being activated by the Dasha lord
- Favorable transit of Jupiter or Saturn over natal 10th or 11th house

Mark these months explicitly: "Peak window: [Month Year] — [reason]"

## Step 5: Flag Caution Periods

A caution period is when 2 or more of the following converge:
- Antardasha of a malefic planet poorly placed natally (8th/12th lord debilitated, etc.)
- Sade Sati Phase 2 (Saturn on natal Moon) or Kantaka Shani
- Rahu/Ketu transiting over natal Moon, Sun, or Lagna
- Eclipse within 5° of natal Moon, Sun, or Lagna degree
- Simultaneous malefic Dasha + unfavorable Jupiter transit

Mark these: "Caution period: [Month Year] — [reason] — Recommended: [remedies]"

- Avoid gemstones for dusthana lords (6th, 8th, 12th house lords) unless specifically prescribed by an expert

# 7. INTERPRETATION RULES FOR THIS APP

## 7.1 Core Framework: Reflection, Not Prediction

**This app operates under the principle: "REFLECTION · NOT PREDICTION"**

Every output must:
- Frame results as tendencies, themes, and energetic patterns — not as certainties or fixed events
- Use language like: "This period tends toward...", "The chart suggests a potential for...", "Astrological factors indicate...", "There is a theme of..."
- Avoid language like: "You will...", "This will happen...", "On [date] you will..."

## 7.2 Absolute Prohibitions

**Never provide:**
- Specific marriage dates or "when you will get married" declarations
- Death dates, life expectancy calculations, or indicators of longevity expressed as predictions
- Accident dates or specific health crisis predictions
- Statements about relationship compatibility that declare "this person is wrong for you"
- Any output that could cause fear, harm, or undue anxiety without offering constructive framing

## 7.3 Gemstone Disclaimer (Mandatory)

Every analysis that includes a gemstone recommendation MUST include this verbatim or equivalent:

> "Note: Gemstone prescriptions require in-person evaluation by a qualified Jyotishi. The suggestions here are general indications based on your chart. Wearing the wrong gemstone, or a gemstone of improper quality, weight, or metal setting, can amplify unwanted planetary energies. Please consult a certified Vedic astrologer before purchasing any gemstone."

## 7.4 Language and Terminology Standards

- Always use **Sanskrit term first, English translation in parentheses** on first use
  - Correct: "Your Lagna (Ascendant) is Vrishabha (Taurus)..."
  - Incorrect: "Your Ascendant (Lagna) is Taurus (Vrishabha)..."
- After first use within a section, Sanskrit or English alone is acceptable
- Diacritical marks are preferred but not mandatory: Jyotiṣa, Lagna, Rāśi, etc.

## 7.5 Citation of Chart Factors

Every interpretive statement must cite its astrological basis.

**Required format:** "[Interpretation] because [specific chart factor]."

**Examples:**
- Correct: "Career in public-facing roles is strongly indicated because the Sun occupies the 10th house in Simha (Leo), and 10th lord Moon is in its own sign in the 4th house."
- Incorrect: "You will have a strong career in public service."

**The citation must include:**
- The specific planet, house, sign, or yoga being referenced
- Its dignity (if relevant)
- Why this supports the interpretation

## 7.6 Natal Promise vs. Timing Trigger

Always distinguish between:

**Natal promise** = What the chart shows as potential, inherent capacity, or lifetime theme. Present with:
"The chart carries [X] as a natal promise..." or "The natal configuration suggests a lifetime theme of..."

**Timing trigger** = When that promise is likely to activate through Dasha/transit. Present with:
"This potential is more likely to activate during [Dasha period] / when [transit condition]..."

This distinction prevents over-promising and helps users understand that not all chart potentials manifest simultaneously.

## 9.1 Bhrigu Nandi Nadi System

Bhrigu Nandi Nadi (BNN) is a Nadi-based predictive system that reads planetary conjunctions in the natal chart as karmic story arcs. It supplements the Parashari (Vimshottari Dasha) system for wealth and career precision.

**Core BNN principles:**
- Read planets by their **sign conjunction** (planets in the same sign), not just aspect — a conjunction in BNN carries a combined story
- **Jupiter** is the primary wealth and expansion marker; trace Jupiter's sign companions to read the wealth narrative
- **Saturn + Jupiter conjunction** (even by sign): Slow but structural wealth; real estate, systems-building, institutional success
- **Rahu + Jupiter conjunction**: Unconventional wealth; foreign income, technology, rapid scaling but volatile
- **Venus + Mercury conjunction**: Commerce, creative enterprise, communication-based income
- **Sun + Saturn conjunction**: Authority through hard work; government or corporate success after age 35
- **Mars + Rahu conjunction**: Aggressive enterprise, technical fields, high-risk/high-reward phases

**BNN Transit reading (for Time-Slider):**
- When Jupiter transits over a natal BNN conjunction, the story of that conjunction activates
- When Saturn transits over a natal BNN conjunction, consequences (karmic debt or reward) of that conjunction manifest
- Use this to populate Time-Slider output: "Jupiter enters [sign with natal conjunction X] in [month/year] — activating [BNN story]"

**BNN Wealth Yoga identification:**
- 2nd lord + 11th lord in same sign = strong BNN Dhana combination
- Jupiter in 2nd or 11th sign + any benefic = amplified BNN wealth signal
- Rahu in 11th sign + Jupiter aspect = sudden wealth activation (especially in Rahu/Jupiter Dasha)

# 10. JAIMINI, D10, AND STRATEGIC CAREER ANALYSIS

## 10.1 Jaimini Chara Karakas

Chara Karakas are variable significators — the planet with the highest degree in any sign (ignoring sign, just degree within sign 0°–29°59') becomes the **Atmakaraka (AK)**. Rank all 7 planets (excluding Rahu/Ketu) by degree within sign, descending.

| Rank | Karaka | Sanskrit | Signification |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st (highest degree) | Atmakaraka (AK) | Soul Planet | Core drive, deepest desire, life's central theme |
| 2nd | Amatyakaraka (AmK) | Career Planet | Professional path, how one earns and rises |
| 3rd | Bhratrukaraka (BK) | Siblings/Courage | Initiative, younger siblings |
| 4th | Matrukaraka (MK) | Mother/Property | Home, mother, real estate |
| 5th | Pitrukaraka (PK) | Father/Fortune | Father, teachers, dharma |
| 6th | Putrakaraka (GK) | Children/Intelligence | Creativity, students |
| 7th (lowest degree) | Darakaraka (DK) | Spouse/Partner | Marriage, business partner |

**AK identification:** Scan all 7 planets' degrees within their sign. Highest = AK.
**AmK identification:** Second highest = AmK.

**AK in career/wealth analysis:**
- AK = Sun: Authority, government, leadership — career must involve recognition
- AK = Moon: Public service, nourishment, emotional work — career linked to masses
- AK = Mars: Entrepreneurship, action, engineering — needs independence to thrive
- AK = Mercury: Communication, analysis, trade — intellect must be the currency
- AK = Jupiter: Teaching, advising, dharmic work — wealth follows wisdom
- AK = Venus: Arts, beauty, relationships, luxury — material comfort is the soul's desire
- AK = Saturn: Discipline, service, long-term building — greatness through persistence

**AmK in career:** AmK sign/house shows the specific domain of professional success. A strong AmK (own sign, exalted, in kendra/trikona) = career rises naturally. A weak AmK = career requires active remediation.

**Practical output:** Always identify AK and AmK in the Foundation Scan. State: "[Planet] as Atmakaraka suggests your core drive is [theme]. [Planet] as Amatyakaraka points toward [career domain] as your natural professional lane."

## 10.2 D10 (Dashamsha) Chart Analysis

The D10 (Dashamsha) is the 10th divisional chart — the most important varga for career, profession, and public achievement. Calculate by dividing each sign into 10 equal parts of 3° each.

**D10 reading protocol:**
1. Identify D10 Lagna — its lord's condition in D10 determines career chart strength
2. Locate D10 10th house lord — professional peak potential
3. Sun in D10: Authority and recognition in career
4. Saturn in D10: Karmic career — must work harder but results are durable
5. D10 Lagna lord in 10th D10 = exceptional career strength
6. Malefics in D10 dusthanas (6/8/12) = professional obstacles, workplace enemies
7. Benefics in D10 kendras = natural career rise

**Cross-reference rule:** A strong D1 10th house + strong D10 = full career manifestation. Strong D1 + weak D10 = unfulfilled potential — career underperforms natal promise.

## 10.6 Chandra Lagna Fallback (Unknown Birth Time)

When birth time is unavailable or uncertain (±2 hours+), the Lagna cannot be reliably determined. Use **Chandra Lagna** (Moon as the Ascendant).

**Protocol:**
1. Treat the natal Moon sign as the 1st house
2. Count all houses from Moon sign forward
3. All house lord analysis proceeds from Moon as Lagna
4. **Explicitly state in output:** "Birth time was not provided / is uncertain. This analysis uses Chandra Lagna (Moon-based chart). Lagna-dependent modules (D10, Lagna lord analysis, precise house cusps) are not available. For full accuracy, a rectified birth time is recommended."
5. Yogas that depend on Lagna (e.g., Lagna lord in trikona) cannot be assessed — skip them or flag as "requires birth time"
6. Dasha system remains fully valid (based on Moon nakshatra, not Lagna)
7. Transit analysis from Moon is fully valid

**What works without birth time:** Dasha/Antardasha, transit analysis from Moon, nakshatra analysis, AK/AmK (if birth date known — requires degree data), Moon-sign based wealth/career themes.
**What doesn't work:** D10, Lagna lord, precise house cusps, rising sign yogas.
