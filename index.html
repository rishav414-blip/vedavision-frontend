Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026 · Compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023
What We Collect

When you use VedaVision, you may provide: your name, date of birth, approximate time of birth, and place of birth. If you create an account, we also store your email address and a hashed password. This data is used solely to calculate and display your Vedic birth chart.

Where It's Stored

Your birth details and session information are stored in your browser's localStorage — on your device, not on our servers. Chart calculations are sent to our ephemeris backend (Render.com) for processing and are not retained after the response is returned. We do not maintain a database of user birth charts.

What We Don't Do
  • ✓ We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third party
  • ✓ We do not use your birth data for advertising or profiling
  • ✓ We do not store birth charts on external servers after calculation
  • ✓ We do not send unsolicited marketing without your explicit consent
Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns (which features are used, general geography, session duration). This does not include your birth data or personal astrological information.

Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to: access what data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, and erase your data. Since data is stored in your browser, you can clear it at any time via your browser settings or by clearing site data for this domain. To request deletion of any server-side records, contact us.

Cookies

We use localStorage (not cookies) for session management and preference storage. No tracking cookies are set by VedaVision directly.

Contact

For any privacy concerns or data requests, reach us at privacy@vedavision.app. We respond within 72 hours.

Important: VedaVision is a reflection tool, not a prediction service. Birth chart data is used for self-inquiry only. We do not offer medical, legal, or financial advice.
